About

Max Boganets is a Russian-born writer living in Illinois. He writes literary and speculative fiction exploring technology, society, and human identity.

The author of eleven peer-reviewed scientific papers, he writes stories about scientific discovery and human consequences, as well as grief, connection, and moving forward.

His short story Those Who Survived appeared in Adelaide Literary Magazine and on Pilcrow.
